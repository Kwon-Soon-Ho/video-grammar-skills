## 해석의 한계

- with_skill=draft.11 candidate; old_skill=current draft.10. Previous iteration used draft.10 vs draft.9; labels are contextual.
- Two known tasks, one execution per version/task. Per-task variation is not repeated-run variance or a general performance estimate.
- source-grounded-2 has nine content/traceability criteria; prior six-criterion scores are preserved and cannot be directly compared as if the rubric were unchanged.
- AI graders can see configuration paths; not blind, not human approval. Process logs are executor self-reports, not complete transcripts.
- Precise model/time/token telemetry is unavailable. No zero, character or timing proxy is presented as measured usage.
- An additional content error is not excused by a high aggregate score. Read source evidence and paired reviews.
- ID-rule interpretation was clarified during development to distinguish reusable-link breaks from harmless prose shorthand. This is not a preregistered benchmark.
- This compares analyzer drafts, not Anthropic creator versus Codex creator.
